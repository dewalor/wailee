<br>
I was the guest artist at a spoken word event organized by Anthony Huen as part of the HKUArts Being Human Festival. My childhood memories of the venue, Villa Haw Par 虎豹別墅, center around the colorful murals with vivid scenes of torture from the eighteen layers of hell.
<br>
![round stained glass entrance with the Chinese characters 虎豹別墅](/images/villa_haw_par_entrance.jpg)
<br>
I arrived an hour early. Professor Koon and Antony told me to walk around and explore the venue. 
<br>
![etchings of animals on black granite](/images/villa_haw_par_entrance_inside.jpg)
<br>
![Red, green and blue block-shaped seats under a chandelier. Red velvet curtains.](/images/villa_haw_par_chandelier_seats.jpg)
<br>
I was relieved to find the murals had been removed. There were more subdued Buddhist-themed etchings on black granite. This appears to be a bodhisattva with companions.
<br>
![etchings of a man in buddhist monk robes with several human companions and a mythical beast on black granite](/images/villa_haw_par_bodhisattva.jpg)
<br>
I'm no expert in Buddhism, so I had to look this up. The Chinese characters 生化 mean "birth" and "transforming."
<br>
![etchings of a snake, a lizard, a crab, a rodent and a shell on black granite, with the Chinese characters 生化, birth and transforming](/images/villa_haw_par_animals.jpg)
<br>
The Chinese characters 生胎 mean "birth" and "womb." 
<br>
![more etchings on black granite: an elephant, an ox, a horse and other mammal-like animals, with the Chinese characters 生胎, birth and womb](/images/villa_haw_par_animals_1.jpg)
<br>
The barely legible Chinese says something like 販賣人家兒女者受刑, crime and punishment for trafficking other people's children.
<br>
![more etchings on black granite: A demon carrying a person and another demon impaling a person with a stake.](/images/villa_haw_par_hell.jpg)
<br>
An evening of authentic poetry, song, dialogue, community and contemplation.  
<br>
![A two-story mansion in the dark, lit from the inside, with white, blue and green stained glass windows and traditional Chinese roofing in the foreground. A taller, modern building with diagonally arranged square tiles and several stories of railing stands behind it.](/images/villa_haw_par_nighttime.jpg)
